Bulk Exporter for ChatGPT – Markdown, JSON, ZIP

A Chrome extension that backs up your ChatGPT conversations in formats you own: clean Markdown plus the original JSON, in one ZIP. You can also search everything you've exported, right in your browser.

  • Free: export any single chat to Markdown + JSON; search exported chats by title.
  • Pro ($5.99/month or $29 lifetime): bulk export your whole history or selected chats to one ZIP, plus full-text search.

Private by design. Your chats go straight from chatgpt.com to your computer. We have no server, analytics, or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
