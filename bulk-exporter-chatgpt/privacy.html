Bulk Exporter for ChatGPT ("the extension") is made by an independent developer. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of Bulk Exporter for ChatGPT. The extension is not affiliated with OpenAI. Contact: matt@flagmylease.com.

What the extension accesses, and why

  • Your chats on chatgpt.com. When you click Load chats or Export in the popup, the extension's script on chatgpt.com requests your conversation list and conversations from the site's own endpoints (chatgpt.com/backend-api/…), using the login you already have in that tab. On ChatGPT it reads the session access token from chatgpt.com/api/auth/session and sends it only back to chatgpt.com. That token is kept in memory only and is never stored or sent anywhere else.
  • Where the data goes. Chats are turned into Markdown and JSON in your browser and saved as files to your computer. Text of exported chats is kept in your browser's IndexedDB storage for this extension so you can search it. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared.
  • Local settings. Export progress and your cached license status are kept in chrome.storage.local.

What we do not do

  • We have no servers that receive your chats, prompts, tokens, or browsing data. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking. We do not sell or transfer user data, and we do not use it for anything other than the export and search features described here.
